Delete your Duckcellent account

You can delete your Duckcellent account and its data yourself at any time, in the Duckcellent app or on the Duckcellent website. It takes about a minute and does not need help from us.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Duckcellent app, or go to duckcellent.vercel.app in any web browser. You do not need to install the app.
  2. Tap Sign in in the top-right corner and sign in with the email and password you used for Duckcellent. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? to get a reset link by email.
  3. Once signed in, tap Account in the top-right corner.
  4. At the bottom of the Your account window, tap Delete account.
  5. Type DELETE (in capital letters) in the box, then tap Delete my account.

A message confirms that your account has been deleted. This cannot be undone.

Can't sign in? Ask us by email

Email admin.duckcellent@gmail.com from the email address of your Duckcellent account, with the subject "Delete my Duckcellent account". We will delete the account and the data listed below within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm the account is yours first.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted straight away, for good

  • Your account: email address and password
  • Your atlas: the countries, states and cities you marked as been, lived or want to go, with any years and notes
  • Your trips: itineraries, flights, stays, booking references, costs and notes
  • Your Duckcellent Plus membership record

If you pay for Duckcellent Plus, the subscription is cancelled immediately before anything is deleted, so you are not charged again. Deleting your account does not refund the current billing period.

Kept for a limited time

  • Payment records (only if you ever paid for Plus): your email, name and billing details, and the payments and invoices. These are held by our payment processor, Stripe, and kept for 5 years from the payment, as Australian tax law requires. Stripe may keep them longer where its own legal obligations require it. Card numbers are held by Stripe only and never reach us.
  • Backups and system logs may still contain your data for up to 7 days before they are overwritten.

Data saved on your own device

If you used Duckcellent without signing in, your atlas and trips are saved only in that browser or on that phone, and we never receive them. To remove them, uninstall the app, or clear the website data for duckcellent.vercel.app in your browser settings.

Want to keep a copy first? Before deleting, tap Account, then Download my data to save your atlas and trips as a file.
